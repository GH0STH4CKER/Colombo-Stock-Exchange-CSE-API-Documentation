# Market Overview
POST  https://www.cse.lk/api/marketStatus                                    Market open/close status (e.g. Regular Trading, Market Closed)
POST  https://www.cse.lk/api/marketSummery                                   Market summary for the day (turnover, share volume, trades)
POST  https://www.cse.lk/api/dailyMarketSummery                              Daily market summary (turnover, foreign/domestic split, PER, PBV, DY, indices)
GET   https://www.cse.lk/api/lastUpdateTime                                  Timestamp of the last market data update
GET   https://www.cse.lk/api/previousUpdateTime                              Previous trading date
POST  https://www.cse.lk/api/cseDaily                                        CSE daily market reports (PDF paths on cdn.cse.lk)
GET   https://www.cse.lk/api/cseDailyNew                                     Latest CSE daily market report
POST  https://www.cse.lk/api/cseWeekly                                       CSE weekly market reports

POST  https://www.cse.lk/api/quarterlySummary                                CSE quarterly market reports
